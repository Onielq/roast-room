# Brief: Taiseel's next 12-month business focus

Mode: full council, eight named seats, three rounds. Missing operating figures are marked unknown, not invented.

## THE BRIEF

**Decision:** What should Taiseel prioritise and sell over the next 12 months?

**Options**
- **A:** Build all four pillars together: Land Intelligence, Development Intelligence, Real Estate Exchange / MLS, and Market Intelligence.
- **B:** Build one paid Land Intelligence product for a narrowly defined business customer, then expand only after demand is proven.
- **C:** Sell a manual, productised site-intelligence service first. Help developers assess specific sites, learn what they pay for, and turn repeated work into software and proprietary data.
- **D:** Stop or pivot away from the current Taiseel direction.

**The business:** Taiseel's broader direction is a Rwanda-first real-estate intelligence and exchange platform. The current product work includes cadastral parcels, zoning, location search, price estimates and property inventory. The separate Taiseel Development website positions the company as a real-estate development and partnership business, including landowner joint ventures, project marketing, escrow coordination and syndication. The website claims 120+ completed projects; the claim is unverified in this review. Its project pages contain estimated building values and sample unit data, not verified transaction evidence.

**Stage and economics:** No reliable current revenue, paying-customer count, gross margin, churn, deal pipeline or customer-acquisition cost was supplied or found in the reviewed project material. Treat these as unknown, not zero.

**Resources:** Team size, founder hours, available cash and runway are unknown. For this review, assume a founder-led, resource-constrained operation with an existing software prototype and no verified repeatable paid demand. (assumed)

**Success:** Within 12 months, prove a repeatable paid offer with a clearly defined buyer, positive delivery economics, reliable evidence and repeat or referral demand. Do not define success as features shipped or map coverage added. (assumed)

**Founder lean:** Not supplied. The council tests all four options without assuming agreement with the existing four-pillar vision.
