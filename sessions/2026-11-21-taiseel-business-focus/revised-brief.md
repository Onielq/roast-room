# Revised brief: free-to-consumer, partner-funded Taiseel

Mode: full council, eight seats, three rounds. This revised brief supersedes the original paid site-intelligence framing.

## Decision

How should Taiseel build a free public property and land discovery platform and earn revenue from professional and institutional partners?

## Options

- **A:** Launch all four pillars and all partner monetisation channels together.
- **B:** Build a phased, free-to-consumer marketplace. Establish reliable public search and dense inventory first, then monetise agents, developers and rental partners through qualified leads and labelled promotion. Add mortgage, professional-service and institutional data products when demand and partner value are proven.
- **C:** Make bespoke paid property/site reports the primary business and productise later.
- **D:** Stop or pivot away from Taiseel.

## The business

Taiseel's long-term architecture has four pillars: Land Intelligence, Development Intelligence, Real Estate Exchange / MLS and Market Intelligence. The intended public user can search property, land, projects and basic location information without paying. Revenue comes from agents, landlords/property managers, developers, banks/mortgage providers and other property-service partners that pay for qualified enquiries, advertising, professional tools, project promotion or institutional data products.

## Stage and economics

The reviewed company material does not establish current public traffic, monthly active users, live/verified listing count, partner contracts, revenue, lead conversion, partner retention or contribution margin. These remain unknown.

## Resources

Cash, runway, team capacity and founder hours remain unknown. Assume constrained early-stage resources for comparison, not as verified figures.

## Success

Prove one repeatable marketplace loop: relevant public search creates qualified enquiries; partners receive real value and renew or pay again; information quality and inventory freshness sustain consumer trust. Public search remains free.

## Founder lean

The founder's clarified lean is B: operate like Zillow, where consumers browse and discover for free, while professional and institutional partners fund the marketplace.
