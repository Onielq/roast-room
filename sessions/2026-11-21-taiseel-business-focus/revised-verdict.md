## Verdict: GO WITH CONDITIONS (Option B)

**The call:** Taiseel should be free to the public and partner-funded. Keep core property and land search free. Build a trusted, current discovery marketplace in a defined Kigali launch market, then monetise professional partners through qualified leads, labelled promotion and useful business tools. Add mortgages, professional services and institutional data products as demand is proven.

This corrected verdict supersedes the first verdict that recommended paid consumer site assessments as the main business model. That model did not reflect the founder's intended platform.

## Why the council chose B

- A free search experience creates the consumer network. Professional partners pay because Taiseel can generate relevant exposure, enquiries or tools. The paying customer is not necessarily the person using the search (Graham, Make Something People Want; Naval, Three Forms of Leverage).
- Lead quality is the product sold to agents, lenders and developers. A large visitor count with weak enquiries does not create a business (Hormozi, Value Equation and Money Model).
- Paid placement must be labelled and must not alter parcel facts, zoning or organic relevance. Trust is an asset, not an ad slot (Bezos, Customer Obsession).
- Do not try to sell to every partner type at launch. Build one consumer discovery loop and one repeatable partner offer first (Martell, Buyback Loop; Wickman, Rocks and Scorecard).
- Free search needs a cost ceiling. The platform should expand only when partner contribution covers data, verification, acquisition and support (Buffett, Dollar-Retained Test).

## Partner revenue model

| Partner | What they can pay for |
|---|---|
| Agents and agencies | Qualified enquiries, territory advertising, professional profiles and optional CRM tools |
| Landlords and rental managers | Rental portfolio subscriptions, featured availability and enquiry tools |
| Developers | Project showcase pages, sponsored campaigns and qualified project enquiries |
| Banks and mortgage providers | Consent-based mortgage enquiries, comparison placements, calculator sponsorships and agreed referral arrangements |
| Insurers, valuers, surveyors, lawyers and other professionals | Verified profiles, service placement and qualified service enquiries |
| Institutional customers | Aggregated market analytics, APIs and workflow tools under separate B2B licences |

These are potential products, not signed contracts or guaranteed revenue.

## Build order

1. **Public marketplace:** free search, listings, parcel/location context, zoning where source-backed, and useful property/project discovery.
2. **Inventory and trust:** current supply, source dates, verification status, clear price labels and stale-listing controls.
3. **First monetisation:** test paid agent/developer/rental partner campaigns only after Taiseel can show relevant enquiries.
4. **Finance and services:** add banks, mortgage providers, insurers and other professionals with explicit user consent and written commercial terms.
5. **Institutional intelligence:** offer paid aggregate analytics/API access without putting basic public search behind a paywall.

The four pillars remain the long-term architecture. They are not four equal launch priorities.

## Conditions

1. Core public browsing and basic property/parcel discovery stay free.
2. Begin in a defined Kigali segment with active, verifiable and recently updated inventory.
3. Start with one or two partner categories, not the entire ecosystem.
4. Clearly label sponsored listings and protect factual information and organic ranking.
5. Share contact details only after clear consent for the specific introduction.
6. Track search-to-contact, lead validity, partner response, conversion, renewal and contribution margin.
7. Keep Taiseel's own development/JV economics separate from the marketplace. Disclose affiliated projects.
8. Do not add major fixed costs or scale paid acquisition until partner renewal and positive unit economics are demonstrated.
9. Mortgage and transaction-linked referral fees need written agreements and local legal review.

## Where they fought

**Free listings or paid listings from day one?** The council favours low-friction basic supply onboarding while inventory density is weak. Paid promotion and lead products should be introduced after there is an audience and meaningful enquiries.

**Lead fees or advertising?** Do not force a single model onto every partner. Prepaid local visibility can suit some agencies, qualified-lead pricing can suit others, and developers may prefer project campaigns. Test one offer at a time.

**Scale or trust?** The seats rejected nationwide expansion as an early target. A smaller marketplace with fresh, trustworthy listings is more valuable than a large map of stale inventory.

**Taiseel as developer and neutral marketplace?** If Taiseel markets its own developments, affiliated projects must be disclosed and ranking standards must remain fair.

## Market evidence

Zillow's FY2025 revenue was $2.583 billion: $1.704 billion Residential (66%), $630 million Rentals (24%), $199 million Mortgages (8%) and $50 million Other (2%). Its investor presentation describes agent and seller offerings, share-of-commission and performance pricing, agent software, builder advertising, mortgage originations and third-party leads, paid rental ads and multifamily subscriptions. Zillow's 2025 10-K states that Premier Agent has prepaid market-based pricing and a pay-for-performance offering, with fees due when a transaction closes with a supplied lead. This confirms the partner-funded mechanics, not a promise that Rwanda can reproduce US economics.

Rwanda examples already show free consumer access with commercial partner-side products. Kivu Listing says buyers browse and contact sellers free while sellers pay for advertising plans and featured placement. Ibyungura promotes paid featured listings. HomesHub advertises free basic listings for agencies and agents. So free browsing is not enough to stand out.

Taiseel's stronger differentiator should be the combination of property listings with location and parcel intelligence: cadastral context, admin search, zoning where verified, nearby services, project data, price provenance and freshness.

## Strongest caution

A free consumer portal with low-quality inventory is not Zillow. It is just another listing site. Taiseel has to earn repeat consumer use first, then prove that partners receive enough qualified opportunities to pay and renew.
