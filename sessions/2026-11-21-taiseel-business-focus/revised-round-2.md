## Chair notes

The board converges on B but adds an important sequence: first create a useful and trusted consumer marketplace in a bounded launch market; next test paid agent/developer/rental partner products; then add mortgage and other financial/service partners. Do not attempt nationwide inventory, every partner category or all four commercial pillars at once.

The strongest risk is marketplace cold start. The second is trust loss through stale listings or undisclosed sponsored ranking. The third is conflicts between Taiseel's own development interests and neutral marketplace rankings.
