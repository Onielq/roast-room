## Chair notes

**Movement:** Bezos, Gary V and Wickman moved from B to C. They accepted that a capped paid service is the fastest test of the buyer and workflow. Graham, Hormozi, Martell and Buffett held C. Naval remains the dissenting product-first voice.

**Cruxes resolved:** The council agrees C is the next experiment, not the final company. C only deserves further investment if it produces repeated customer demand, positive delivery economics and reusable data. B is the next stage if the test clears. The council rejects A because it multiplies customer types, operating models and capital demands before one is proven.

**Open facts:** Revenue, cash, runway, team capacity, actual inventory, data licences, legal permissions and price willingness remain unverified.
