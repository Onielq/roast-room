# Draft resolution: free search, partner-funded marketplace

## Recommendation: Option B

Taiseel should operate as a free public property and land discovery platform. Consumers should be able to browse, search, compare, inspect basic parcel/location information and contact providers without paying Taiseel for access.

The revenue engine sits on the professional side. Agents, developers, landlords/property managers, mortgage providers and other property services pay for verified exposure, qualified introductions, professional workflows or institutional tools. Do not charge every partner in the same way.

## Product sequence

**Stage 1: Build the consumer reason to return**
- Reliable searchable inventory in a bounded Kigali launch market.
- Free core search and property/parcel pages.
- Strong location intelligence: district/sector/cell/village navigation, parcel context, zoning where source-backed, nearby services and project context.
- Listing status, verification level, source and last-updated date.
- Clear distinction between asking prices, observed transactions, official reference values and modelled estimates.

**Stage 2: Monetise the partner side**
- Agents/agencies: qualified leads, territory campaigns, professional profiles and optional CRM tools.
- Developers: showcase pages, promoted new projects and enquiry campaigns.
- Landlords/property managers: rental portfolio packages, enquiry tools and featured availability.
- Start with one or two segments. Do not launch every partner package simultaneously.

**Stage 3: Add adjacent financial and professional services**
- Banks/mortgage providers: consent-based home-loan enquiries, comparison placements, calculators and agreed referral economics.
- Insurers, valuers, surveyors, lawyers and other professionals: service discovery and qualified leads.
- Institutions: aggregated analytics, APIs and workflow tools under separate B2B agreements.

## Trust rules

1. Core consumer search stays free.
2. Paid placements are clearly labelled and do not alter parcel facts, zoning, market estimates or organic relevance.
3. Basic supply onboarding can be free while inventory density is low. Monetise premium exposure and qualified outcomes after value is proven.
4. A user's contact details are shared with a partner only for a clearly consented introduction.
5. Every lead has a quality definition. Track invalid, duplicate, unanswered and converted leads.
6. Every listing has an update date and freshness status. Expired or unavailable inventory must be removed or flagged.
7. If Taiseel has an affiliated development project, disclose the relationship and keep its organic ranking fair.
8. Public search is not a substitute for land title verification, legal advice, a valuation or bank approval.

## First commercial experiment

Recruit a small cohort of agents/developers in one launch area. Provide free basic listings initially. Once the platform can demonstrate relevant consumer enquiries, test one partner offer at a time:
- prepaid, clearly labelled promotion by area/category; or
- qualified lead pricing; or
- post-completion/performance pricing under a written agreement.

Do not assume a bank will pay just because it has a mortgage product. First agree on the customer segment, consent, minimum lead data, qualification criteria, response time, attribution and commercial terms.

## Scorecard

Track the marketplace, not vanity alone:
- active, verified and recently updated listings;
- share of searches that return useful results;
- search-to-contact and contact-to-viewing rates;
- partner response time and invalid/duplicate lead rate;
- qualified leads by category and area;
- partner conversion, renewal and revenue per partner;
- revenue per visit and contribution after data, verification, sales and support costs;
- repeat consumer visits and successful return-to-search behaviour.

## Risks to test

- Cold start: visitors will not return without useful, current inventory; partners will not pay without useful enquiries.
- Accuracy: zoning and price estimates must show source, date and confidence.
- Partner trust: lead quality must be good enough to justify renewal.
- Neutrality: paid ranking and Taiseel-owned projects must not undermine public trust.
- Privacy and regulation: lead sharing, mortgage referrals and any transaction-linked fees need explicit consent, written agreements and local legal review.

## Decision rule

Keep the public side free. Keep the partner revenue side measurable. Scale only the partner product that demonstrates repeat payment and positive contribution. Maintain all four pillars as the long-term architecture, but phase development according to consumer use and partner demand.
