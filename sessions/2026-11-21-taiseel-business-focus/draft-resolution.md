# Draft resolution: paid site-intelligence pilot

## Recommendation: Option C, with a defined path to B

For the next 90 days, Taiseel should sell one fixed-scope, paid site-intelligence assessment to one customer type: Kigali-based real-estate developers evaluating a specific parcel for acquisition or development.

Use existing maps and internal tools to deliver the service manually. Do not expand all four pillars or build a full MLS at this stage. The service is a customer-discovery and cash-generating test, not the permanent business model. If repeated demand is proven, productise the common workflow as the paid Land Intelligence product (Option B).

## The offer

**Working name:** Taiseel Site Decision Brief.

For one parcel identified by UPI or coordinates, deliver a concise, sourced assessment covering:
- parcel geometry and area, with source and confidence;
- planned land use and zoning, with source date and applicable limitations;
- road access and nearby services;
- development context and relevant nearby projects;
- comparable market information only where the source and date are defensible;
- material unknowns, data gaps and items requiring independent professional or official verification.

This is decision support, not a land-title certificate, legal due diligence, survey, valuation, planning approval or guarantee of buildability. Never present indicative market estimates as verified asking prices or transaction prices.

## Conditions before further investment

1. **Buyer test:** Complete 20 conversations with qualified Kigali developers or their acquisition leads within the first 30 days.
2. **Paid proof:** Secure at least five paid pilot assessments within 90 days. No free custom reports count as demand.
3. **Repeat signal:** At least two clients place a second order, request a retainer, or refer another paying client.
4. **Delivery economics:** Track every delivery hour and direct cost. By the fifth paid report, target direct delivery costs below 50% of the collected fee.
5. **Data quality:** Every material claim must show its source, date and confidence. High-impact unknowns must be visible. No unsupported claims of title, ownership, legal compliance or market value.
6. **Founder capacity:** Do not hire or add fixed costs during the test. Cap the scope to available time and cash.

## 90-day execution

- **Days 1-14:** Define the report template, data-source register, exclusions and pilot offer. Interview and approach ten target buyers.
- **Days 15-30:** Complete the 20 buyer conversations. Ask for a live site decision and payment before doing the work.
- **Days 31-60:** Deliver the first three paid reports. Record questions, corrections, time, cost, buyer use and objections.
- **Days 61-90:** Reach five paid reports, seek repeat orders, calculate contribution margin and decide whether to productise.

## Separate the businesses

Taiseel Development's property development, landowner joint-venture, escrow and syndication activities must have separate project underwriting, contracts, cash accounting and risk approvals from the Land Intelligence software activity. Do not use an unproven software product to imply a development track record, or development marketing claims as software traction.

The public claim of 120+ completed projects must be backed by a verifiable project list and evidence before it is used as proof. Review sample project pages for placeholder or stale data, particularly estimated building values, USD-per-square-foot figures and unit histories.

## Cheapest test

Offer a fixed-scope assessment to ten developers with a real parcel decision. Ask for a paid booking or deposit before doing the research. Deliver the first report manually.

## Kill or reshape criteria

Stop or reshape the offer if, after 20 qualified buyer conversations, fewer than three clients will pay; if reports do not change a real decision; if direct delivery costs stay above 70% of fees after five reports; or if the required data cannot be obtained and used lawfully and reliably.

## Decision after 90 days

If the paid and repeat-demand conditions clear, build only the workflow that repeated buyers used, with a paid B2B Land Intelligence offer. If customers value bespoke judgement but not a standard product, keep the service small and profitable or change the offer. If customers will not pay, stop this wedge rather than adding more features.
